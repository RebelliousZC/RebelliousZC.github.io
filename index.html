Becoming Itsuki Fujii: A Student Production of Love Letter 《情书》：在镜头前成为藤井树

I played the male lead, Itsuki Fujii, in a production of Love Letter created by students from the Department of Media.

参演传媒系同学创作的《情书》，饰演男主藤井树，第一次走进较为专业的拍摄现场。

Playing Itsuki Fujii took me well beyond my familiar life as an engineering student. Watching a film and standing in front of a camera turned out to be very different experiences. Making a gesture or a pause feel natural was a challenge in itself, and stepping into a character asked for a kind of attention I had rarely used before.

This was my first experience of a more professional filming process. Being on set gave me a much clearer understanding of the work behind a finished scene and a deeper appreciation of the patience and coordination that filmmaking requires.

Just as memorable was the time spent with the student crew. Working together was genuinely enjoyable. I came away with new respect for their craft, and with the pleasure of having tried something far outside my usual field.

对一个习惯了课堂和实验室的工科生来说，饰演藤井树是一次很新鲜、也很有挑战的尝试。坐在银幕前看故事，与站在镜头前成为故事里的人，原来是两种完全不同的体验。一个动作、一处停顿，怎样才显得自然，都需要重新体会。

这是我第一次接触较为专业的拍摄过程。真正参与其中，才更清楚地感受到，一段看似平静流畅的画面背后，需要多少耐心、配合与投入。拍摄的不易也让我对演员和摄影团队的工作有了更具体、更深的认识。

和团队同学相处的过程非常愉快。大家一起完成一段影像，也一起留下了镜头之外的记忆。能够暂时走出熟悉的专业，认真尝试另一种创作，本身就很值得。

Ballroom dancing had rarely been part of the world I knew as an engineering student. Joining a dance evening made that world feel suddenly larger. There was the music, the movement, and the simple pleasure of finding a rhythm with another person.

What appealed to me was the way dancing draws attention into the present: listening, moving, and responding to a partner. It offered a very different kind of enjoyment from my usual routine, and made me curious about an interest I had scarcely considered before.

These photographs keep a few moments from the dance floor. More than anything, they remind me how much there still is to try. Sometimes a new interest begins with a small surprise: I had no idea this could be so much fun.

在我熟悉的工科生活圈里，跳舞并不是一件经常被谈起的事。真正参加舞会之后，才发现音乐、舞步和两个人之间的配合，能够带来如此直接的快乐。原本觉得离自己很远的世界，一下子变得可以亲身参与。

这里留下的是舞会中的几个片段。比起跳得如何，我更珍惜那种发现新鲜事物的兴奋——原来生活里还有这么有意思的事情，值得自己走近一点，再试一次。

For the National Day holiday, I brought together a group of friends and classmates for a hike through Jiuyao and Yuhuang Hills. People I usually met on campus became companions on the trail, sharing a day of walking and views across Hangzhou.

趁着国庆假期，我组织了几位平时一起相处的朋友和同学，去九曜山、玉皇山走一走。熟悉的人从校园来到山路上，换一个场景相处，连平常的闲聊也多了几分轻松。

回头看，最喜欢的恰恰是这件事的简单：约上几个熟悉的人，选一条路，就能够拥有一个不同于日常的假日。山路走完了，那份共同经历的快乐却留了下来。

A lively Mid-Autumn market brought lanterns, folk traditions, and the warmth of a shared celebration vividly to life.

在灯火、人声与传统民俗之间，遇见一个几乎从梦里走出来的中秋。

Walking into the Mid-Autumn market felt like entering a scene I had imagined long before seeing it. Lanterns glowed among the trees, lights shimmered on the water, and the streets were full of people. The liveliness of it all was unexpectedly moving.

Here, a traditional festival was something I could experience with all my senses. The folk customs, the gathering crowds, and the shared excitement gave the celebration a warmth that felt wonderfully real. For a moment, I found myself close to tears.

What stayed with me was the feeling that a dreamlike image of the festival had become tangible. I could walk through it, stand among the lights, and be part of the evening. These photographs hold a little of that atmosphere.

走进中秋集市时，有一种梦中场景突然落到眼前的恍惚。树间的灯笼、水面的光影、街巷里来往的人群，把热闹连成了一片。原来心里一直向往的节日，可以这样具体地出现在现实里。

最打动我的，是传统民俗与人间烟火真正融在一起的感觉。身处其中，节日不再只是日历上的一个名字，而是能够看见、听见、亲身参与的相聚。那一刻甚至有些热泪盈眶：这就是我想象过的中秋氛围。

我把这一晚记作一次梦境的具象化。那些原本只能想象的灯火与欢腾，终于有了可以走进去的街道，也留下了属于自己的体验和记忆。

Walking Through Hangzhou: Zhejiang University Spring Hike 用脚丈量杭城：浙江大学春季毅行

I completed a 22.9-kilometer spring hike across Hangzhou, trading the usual campus routine for nearly eight hours of mountain paths and city views.

完成22.9公里春季毅行，用近八小时走过山路与城市风景。

Read more Show less 展开阅读 收起

The route connected campus, wooded hills, distant skylines, and views across West Lake. Places that usually appeared as names on a map gradually became slopes, turns, stretches of shade, and changing horizons. Walking made the city feel both larger and more intimate.

Over 22.9 kilometers, 38,617 steps, and nearly eight hours, there were steep climbs and long stretches when the finish still felt far away. The day became an exercise in pacing: moving steadily, adjusting when tired, and allowing small milestones to carry me toward the next one.

Reaching the end brought more than the satisfaction of a new personal best. The hike offered a slower way to experience Hangzhou and a reminder that endurance is rarely dramatic. It is built through ordinary steps, repeated patiently until they become a journey.

路线串联起校园、林木掩映的山岭、远处的城市天际线与西湖风景。地图上熟悉的地名，在脚步中逐渐变成坡度、转弯、树荫与不断变化的地平线。以步行的方式重新认识杭州，城市显得更加辽阔，也更加亲近。

22.9公里、38,617步、近八小时的行程中，既有陡峭的上坡，也有终点仿佛仍在远方的漫长路段。这也是一次关于节奏的练习：保持稳定，在疲惫时及时调整，再用一个个小目标把自己带到下一程。

抵达终点带来的不只是刷新个人纪录的满足。毅行让我以更缓慢的方式感受杭州，也再次提醒我，耐力往往并不轰轰烈烈，而是由寻常的一步又一步耐心累积，最终连成一段完整的旅程。
